## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for an intimate, celebratory, and academic milestone: a university graduation invitation suite designed with feminine grace, sophistication, and academic prestige. The aesthetic sits at the intersection of refined editorial publishing and modern tactile luxury. 

The emotional tone balances accomplishment, warmth, nostalgic pride, and forward-looking optimism. Visual tension is achieved by pairing delicate blush textures and shimmering champagne tones with deep, authoritative midnight navy typography, delivering both celebratory warmth and scholarly stature. Layouts embrace generous negative space, fine hairline borders reminiscent of bespoke stationery, and subtle dual-toned highlights that mimic hot-stamped gold foil against soft cotton-pressed paper.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from classic stationery, academic regalia, and fine champagne:

- **Primary (`#D4AF37` - Champagne Gold):** Serves as the celebratory accent, featured in monograms, ornamental flourishes, key call-to-actions, badges, and gilded dividers. An elevated tint (`#F3E5AB`) functions as a subtle background glow or interactive hover tint.
- **Secondary (`#1E293B` - Deep Midnight Navy):** Provides editorial contrast, legibility, and grounding formality. Applied to dominant text, headings, structural container borders, and formal status elements.
- **Tertiary (`#F7E7E2` - Warm Blush Petal):** Imparts warmth, gentleness, and feminine elegance. Used as secondary surface containers, soft pill chips, and card backdrop variations.
- **Neutral (`#FFF9F5` - Warm Cream Silk):** The canvas foundational surface, offering a soft, warm paper feel instead of stark digital white.

Pairing rule: Text should always utilize deep midnight navy against cream and blush backgrounds to maintain high legibility and refined editorial contrast. Gold is strictly reserved for titles, accent icons, thin framing borders, and interactive highlights.

## Typography

The typographical hierarchy is structured around two complementary voices:

1. **Editorial Academic Heritage (`Playfair Display`):** Utilized for ceremonial headers, graduate honors, formal proclamations, and personal names. It carries classical letterforms, high contrast between thick and thin strokes, and a stately air befitting academic commencement.
2. **Contemporary Clarity (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Delivers clean, humanized legibility for logistical details including addresses, chronological schedules, formal dress codes, map directions, and RSVP forms.

All uppercase labels (dates, badges, and RSVP steps) utilize generous letter spacing (`letterSpacing: 0.08em` to `0.12em`) to emulate letterpress typesetting. Body copy maintains generous line heights to preserve breathing room and quiet elegance.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy centers on a fixed, structured digital canvas mirroring luxury physical stationery (such as 5x7 inch or A5 ceremonial cards) that expands gracefully across viewport sizes.

- **Grid Model:** A centered 12-column adaptive layout with a max container width of 720px for singular invitation cards, and up to 1080px for interactive multi-column celebration suites (ceremony timeline, campus directions, photo memories, and guest RSVP).
- **Mobile Adaptations (< 640px):** Single-column stacked presentation with `margin: 1.5rem` and `gutter: 1.25rem`. Margins ensure card frames don't hug viewport edges.
- **Tablet & Desktop (≥ 768px):** Expands to multi-pane split layouts (e.g., formal invitation panel on the left, ceremony itinerary and map on the right) with `margin-lg: 4.5rem` and generous `space-xl` between sections to avoid digital density.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows an intentional breath-in, breath-out cadence: dense typographic clusters for date/time details are flanked by expansive `space-lg` and `space-xl` margins.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through delicate, tactile realism rather than heavy digital layering, evoking hot foil stamping on premium, multi-ply cotton paper:

- **Surface Layers:** The root background uses `#FFF9F5` (Warm Cream). Elevated card surfaces sit on `#FFFFFF` with ultra-fine borders: `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.28)` (champagne hairline framing).
- **Tonal Shadows:** Shadows are warm-tinted and deeply diffused to simulate paper resting on linen: `box-shadow: 0 12px 36px -8px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`.
- **Gilded Borders & Insets:** Double-border motifs (a primary card border enclosed within a 1px dashed or hairline gold inset frame spaced 6px apart) are used on primary invitation envelopes and certificate summaries.
- **Glass & Frosting:** For modal popovers or RSVP bottom sheets, a subtle backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) with `rgba(255, 249, 245, 0.88)` preserves background warmth while focusing guest attention.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Soft (Level 1)** curvature language. Subtle corner radii (0.25rem / 4px on interactive controls, up to 0.75rem / 12px on primary cards) maintain the crisp, precise geometry of cut stationery and formal envelopes, avoiding overly casual, bubbly aesthetics.

Pill shapes are selectively applied only to small status tags and timeline milestone nodes to evoke ribbon seals and wax seals.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary (RSVP / Confirm Attendance):** Solid `#1E293B` background with `#FFF9F5` typography, framed with an optional 1px border in `#D4AF37`. On hover, transitions smoothly to a deep gold tint `#D4AF37` with `#1E293B` text. Padding: `0.75rem 2rem`. Text in `label-lg` uppercase.
- **Secondary / Outline (Add to Calendar, View Map):** Transparent background with `1px solid #D4AF37`, text colored `#1E293B`. Soft hover state shifts background to `#F7E7E2`.
- **Tertiary / Link (Decline with Regret):** Text-only `#1E293B` with a subtle gold underline (`text-decoration-color: #D4AF37; text-underline-offset: 4px;`).

### 2. Cards & Frames
- **Ceremonial Invitation Card:** Off-white background (`#FFFFFF`) set over `#FFF9F5`, featuring an outer 1px border in `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)` and a delicate secondary inner border inset by `8px`. Padding: `2.5rem` desktop, `1.5rem` mobile.
- **Schedule / Itinerary Cards:** Layered `#F7E7E2` background, separated by gold hairline rules (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)`), highlighting timestamps in `Playfair Display` alongside concise description text.

### 3. Chips & Badges
- **Honors & Dress Code Chips:** Border radius `rounded-full` with a light blush background (`#F7E7E2`), a refined `1px solid #D4AF37` outline, and tracking-expanded text in `label-md`.

### 4. Form Inputs (RSVP Submission)
- **Text Fields:** Minimalist single-line or soft container inputs with a bottom-border emphasis or `1px solid rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.15)`. On focus, transitions to `1px solid #D4AF37` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`). Placeholder styled in muted slate.
- **Checkboxes & Radios (Attending / Dietary Preferences):** Custom circular controls with a `1.5px solid #D4AF37` perimeter. Selected state fills with `#1E293B` and reveals a delicate champagne checkmark.

### 5. Timeline & Schedule Component
- Vertical thin stem rendered in `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`. Each event node is marked by a dual-ring circular point (`6px` solid `#D4AF37` center with an outer `14px` ring in `#F7E7E2`).

### 6. Envelope & Monogram Seal
- A stylized circular crest hosting the graduate's initials in `Playfair Display`, rendered with a radial champagne gold gradient and fine drop shadow to simulate a custom embossed wax seal.
